Seeds of Future | Interactive Installation @ DICF

Delhi International Children's Festival ( DICF ) is an annual event that bring children and youth from 24 countries to celebrate diversity and freedom of expression through performing and visual arts.The organisers of the event had invited installation proposals an that's when I had an opportunity to collaborate with a brilliant Architect-designer Sushant Rai. Together we took 2 weeks to propose a design for an installation called "Seeds of future".
